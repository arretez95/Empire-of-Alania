Alanue is situated at the mouths of rivers Naros and Foruks and is its most populated city.

The city is encircled by three walls. The areas between them are known as Rings. The Outer Ring is the most populous, inhabited by peasants and the working class. The Inner Ring is populated by the nobility. Outer Ring inhabitants are allowed into the Inner ring for work purposes only. In the center of the kingdom is The Royal Grounds, which can only be entered with explicit permission from the ruling power.

**History:**
*Raiding Era*
A few thousand years ago, the Ayans settled by the mouths of rivers Naros and Foruks, taking advantage of its fertile soils and teeming fisheries.

They were subject to repeated raids by the Norts, nomadic tribes from the northern plains. After a particularly brutal skirmish where half the city was set alight, the Ayans were forced into a treaty requiring them to provide the tribes women of marrying age in exchange for the tribe's protection. This would would develop into the current-day Reaping Festival, where all young men and women of marrying age would come together to choose their partners. Ayan-No, as the city became known as, became the tribes' meeting grounds. To this day, nomadic tribes from the region will gather in Alanue for the day of the Reaping.

*Millas-Alanue War*
An nasty encounter with the Millas tribe left an entire hunting party mortally wounded with one survivor. The details of the encounter are unknown, only that it launched a feud for several hundred years. During this time, Alanuans came into contact with Xxtani merchants who had jewels from Zarenia. The Alanuans, having no stonework expertise, largely made jewelry out of wood and seashells and were fascinated by the jewels.

One of the jewels was heavily imbued with natural energy. And when the person picked up the jewel, it fired a blast of air, sending them flying backward. And the slaughter began. After securing several shiploads of Zarenian gems, the Alanuans drove the Millas into camps, to which they still live this day.

Paranoid of further attacks from their neighbors, the Alanuans also pushed eastward into Valviceno. When Valviceno fell, they moved onto Aca. Then Zarenia.




Nestled gracefully beside sparkling ocean waves, this magnificent medieval city stands as a testament to architectural brilliance and grandeur. Gazing out from its towering walls, one is captivated by the breathtaking beauty that surrounds this metropolis. With its narrow cobblestone streets, adorned with vibrant flowers and bustling market stalls, Alanue exudes an inviting charm that welcomes locals and visitors alike.
 
The salty ocean breeze mingles with the aromas of freshly baked bread and savory spices, enticing wanderers to lose themselves in maze-like alleys. From the impressive castle guarding the city's heart to the ornate cathedrals reaching towards the heavens, Alanue is a tapestry of architectural wonders. The sound of lapping waves provides a soothing soundtrack as ships bob willingly in the harbor, laden with exotic treasures from distant lands. Its warm-hearted inhabitants foster a sense of community and belonging, ensuring that all who enter feel like cherished guests. With each passing day, Alanue's rich history and breathtaking vistas reminded its residents of the majesty that lay at its doorstep, intertwining the allure of the medieval and the enchantment of the ocean in perfect harmony.
 
But underneath its warm facade is a long history of war and expansion. It has a longstanding feud with Zarenia. In the past few decades, Zarenians, falsely promised with new business and educational opportunities, were taken back to the mainland and pressed into slavery. This sparked a trend among wealthy Alanuans to designate highly desirable native Zarenians as "wild-caught slaves" versus what they considered physically weaker Alanuan "bred" slaves.

The poor treatment of their diaspora sparked a renewed Zarenian revolution under the banner Promise of Freedom . Expelling remaining Alanuan The Guardians , Zarenians sought to regain their previous sovereignty. Alanuans are now only permitted to dock in Casbar with restricted access to the port city.

### Government
The entire city is surrounded in layers of walls, whose gates can only be opened or closed by Earth Guardians.

### Infrastructure
Alanue is structured into three sections: the Outer Ring, the Upper Ring, and the Inner Ring. Each ring is connected by canals which transport goods and people.

**Outer Ring**

Composed mostly of peasants and other "undesirables".

 **Upper Ring**

Consists of the middle class and some wealthy nobles.

 **Inner Ring**

The location of the King's palace and important or incredibly wealthy nobility.
