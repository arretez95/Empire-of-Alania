The **Alanue Military Academy**, or simply, the Academy, can trace its history back to the days of Alanue's rivalry with Millas. Fortifications built to defend against a naval assault make up the outer walls of the campus. After the union with Millas, the fort was restructured to accommodate new military recruits.

The Academy's original aim was to provide military men with training. This evolved to training cadets into military officers, providing instruction in all general courses before students test into more specialized branches of the curriculum.

Admission into the Academy is granted by recommendation from the Alanuan Royal Family or by a military officer of high rank.

**Features**
First-time students and visitors are greeted on arrival by a massive stone gate.
