Smelting zanite together with gemstones was how Alanuans unlocked elemental abilities. Maneuvering one's hands a certain way would press a combination of switches and trigger certain spells.

Unlike Zarenian spirits, which grant unique powers depending on both the species and individual, elemental abilities derived from crystal are uniform and does not require casting time, making them easier to teach. Their power differs only in the quality of both the crystal and the construction of the bracer.

However, once depleted, the crystal is suitable only for decoration as it has a finite amount of energy. Abilities must also cast directly from the crystal, leaving the user with potential openings if they are too focused on their spellcasting.
## Elemental Stones
### [[Altaneon|Zanalteon]]
*wind crystal - (zanite + altaneon) - allows a user to emit powerful gusts.*
#### Air Manipulation
| **Spell**       | **Type**    | **Movement**                                        | **Description**                                                                                         |
| --------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gust**        | Fundamental | 1st Position: Press four fingers against the thumb. | a burst of air strong enough to knock someone to the ground.                                            |
| **Flow**        | Fundamental | 2nd Positon: Form a fist.                           | Directs the flow of natural energy.                                                                     |
| **Gale**        | Spell       | 3rd Position: Press both hands together             | A sustained wind. Used for repelling, reflecting, or increasing speed.                                  |
| **Wind Bullet** | Spell       | 4th Position: Punch with a fist.                    | A ball of compressed air that can be used for knockdown and causing damage. A stronger version of Gust. |

| **Spell**  | **Type**    | **Movement**                                                                            | **Description **                                                                                                |
|------------|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Growth** | Fundamental | 1st Position: Palm outstretched, wave arms up and down.                                 | accelerates the growth of a plant. The older or the more unknown the plant, the more energy needed to grow it.  |
| **Shape**  | Fundamental | 2nd Position: Outstretched palm slightly curled, turn wrist in the direction of growth. | controls the shape of a growing plant. The older or more unknown the plant, the more energy needed to shape.    |
| **Bloom**  | Spell       | Move back to 1st Position from 2nd Position.                                            | Forces flowers to bloom.                                                                                        |
| **Decay**  | Spell       | Wave hands toward the chest                                                             | Accelerate the lifespan of the plant.                                                                           |
