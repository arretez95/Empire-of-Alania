### Introduction
Princess Raewynn is the heir apparent to the imperial throne. 

The youngest child of the Emperor and his first wife and is the only surviving child from this marriage. On her third birthday, as per royal tradition, she was gifted her slave companion, who she named [[Brandon Carona|Russell]], to act as her confidant and guard.
