### Government
Previously, the country maintained standards through collective agreements between cities as travel throughout the country is difficult. Zarenia semi-centralized its government only recently i.e. after the rebellion.

The Zarenian capital remains mobile under the wishes of the First Chief Brandon "Chase" Carona. It rotates between the country's three major cities of Casbar, Dunsterton, and Redcker. Brandon believes the government should see how its people are living as it moves through the countryside instead of the former Alanuan style of directing from afar.
