The **Port of Casbar** is the main gateway into Zarenia. Although subject to the whims of town council, it is largely self-managed. It is the largest seaport in Zarenia and is responsible for the majority of the cargo entering and exiting the country.
### History
Zalens inhabited the area now known as Casbar long before the city's current prominence. They were peaceful people and skilled in the art of fishing, hunting, and stone carving.

Casbar's growth began when a band of Açene sailors were blown off course during a storm and ended up on Zalen's shores. Fascinated by the Zalens' skill with fish bone and stone carvings, the sailors established a trading post and named the settlement for the indigenous people they encountered.

Word of Zaren spread, and the Zalens  embraced this new era, the promises of new trade and technology enticing. The port flourished, bustling with activity as ships from around the world docked and unloaded their wares.

Zaren evolved from this modest trading post into a thriving seaport. Its streets, now lined with warehouses, taverns, and shops, cater to the ever-growing needs of the community. With each passing decade, the seaport expanded, shipbuilding flourished, and Zaren became renowned for its sturdy vessels, crafted by master shipwrights. The town's economy thrived as it established trade routes with Alanue and Xxtan, bringing in spices, fabrics, and precious metals.

However, Zaren experienced periodic setbacks. Fierce storms battered its shores and left countless ships stranded on its reefs. Wars from across the ocean arrived on its shores, bringing death and destruction to its people. Yet each time, Zaren's resilience and unwavering determination prevailed, and the town rebuilt itself stronger each time.

Recently, during the Era of Conquest, Alanuans conquered the city despite heavy resistance. Overwhelmed by their opponents' superior technology and numbers and cutoff from their supply lines, the Zalens surrendered quickly. The Alanuan's commanding general renamed the city after Gauvain Casbar, one of Alanue's greatest warriors. He felt the Zalen's quick surrender was distasteful and cast them off as weak.
 
Decades later would be the final standoff between the Promise and the Guardians and the Casbarian Massacre.

Today, the Port of Casbar stands as a testament to the indomitable spirit of its people and its enduring connection between land and sea. Its picturesque harbor and storied past continue to draw visitors from near and far, reminding all who visit of the rich tapestry woven by centuries of growth and transformation.
