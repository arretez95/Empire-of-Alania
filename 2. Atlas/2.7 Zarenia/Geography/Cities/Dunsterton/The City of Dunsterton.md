Dunsterton is the main naval base of Zarenia, located by the Swaddle of Zalen. The base was established for defensive purposes, and every part of the city is heavily fortified.
### Demographics
At one point during the rebellion, the city became a haven for pirates, whose activities drove away timid Zarenians and encouraged settlers of more heinous purposes. Daniel assumed his position as commander in the Promise, he prioritized getting pirates and smugglers under his wing. In return for their harassment of Alanuan ships, he gave them permission to dock in Dunsterton so long as they paid the necessary levies.

To this day, Dunsterton is a mishmash of various nationalities, with the most common languages being Alanuan, Aça, and Xxtani.
### Government
Like Casbar, Dunsterton dislikes any form of extreme autocracy. However, the leadership is more susceptible to bribery and votes can be bought.
### Defences
After Brandon is appointed Chief of Zarenia, he convinces some pirates to form the new nation's first naval defensive forces.A series of harbor chain nets between each inlet prevent the threat of invasion. Rows of battlements line the shores of the bay, and along with patrolling naval ships, keep guard day and night.
### Industry & Trade
With resources shipped from the mining town of Wargham and the lumber mills of Tetford, Dunsterton is headquarters to Zarenia's heavy industry. All kinds of minerals, jewels, and other raw materals can be found for sale here. The Zarenian government also funds research and development of defense systems, making Dunsterton the center of Zarenian development.
