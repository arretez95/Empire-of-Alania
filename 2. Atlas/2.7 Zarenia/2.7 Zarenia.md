**Zarenia** is a country in Eastern [[Alania]]. It makes up the southern half of the Zarenian Peninsula and shares its only land border with [[Aça]]. Its western border is formed by the [[Sea of Zaren]], and to its east the ocean of [[Tempests' Rage]].

### Government
Previously, the region maintained standards through collective agreements between cities as travel through the country is difficult. Zarenia semi-centralized its government only after the [[rebellion]].

Representatives from each of the six regions under Alanuan occupation gathered for a conference to discuss the fate of the Zarenia during and after the war. It was agreed that to prevent future domestic and economic takeover by foreign powers, a central figure was needed to voice and negotiate Zarenian concerns. Brandon was chosen for his levelheadedness and assertiveness. He was also the only recommended delegate who initially refused to join the delegation due to conflicting interest—he was born in Alanue.

##### Executive Branch
The executive administration, composed of [[The Council of Seven]], remains mobile under the wishes of the First Chieftain [[Brandon Carona]]. The administrative government rotates between the country's three major cities: [[The City of Casbar|Casbar]], [[The City of Dunsterton|Dunsterton]], and [[The City of Redcker|Redcker]]. Brandon believes the government should see how its people are living as it moves through the countryside instead of the former Alanuan style of directing from afar.
