**Human:** [[Brandon Carona|Brandon]]
**Species:** [[Fungal Spore Spirit]]
**Element:** <span style="color:#7AC74C;">Plant</span>
**Ability:** Mycorrhiza

If you feed her, you're toast. Espor will do *anything* for food. On one hand, this makes her relatively easy to work with. But once she gets hungry...

This talking green mushroom has only one desire in this world: to eat. While berries are her upmost favorite, she certainly won't turn down a bite of juicy steak. This puts her human in a conundrum because he now has to do the one thing he hates: sharing food.

She has an aversion to jerky, smoked meats, and salt.

They say Spirits are born from animals, plants, and other creatures that have absorbed large amounts of Zalen's essence. Since Espor gained her consciousness, like all fungi, all she knew was "eat". It wasn't until that fateful day she got involved with [[Curtidagua]] and [[Diaroca]] that her life changed for the better. All of a sudden, she didn't have to find food by herself. She could make Brandon do it *for* her! And he never turned down her requests.

They discovered how to use [[Magic Style — Spirit Power|Spirit Power]] when Brandon was ambushed by a troop of Guardians. In her shock, Espor accidentally released a burst of Spirit Power while hanging off Brandon's shoulder. Brandon subconsciously absorbed the energy, blooming a single stalk of bamboo in front of a sword-swinging Guardian. The tip of the stalk rammed into the Guardian's jaw, knocking him unconscious.
