Brandon is the first chieftain of Zarenia and an abolitionist. As leader of the Redcker chapter of the Zarenian rebellion, after pushing out Alanuan Guardians, he was responsible for networking secret routes and safehouses to Redcker.

Born into slavery in Alanue, Brandon was subject to execution at the age of eight, but escaped death and was pulled ashore by a Montafielian fisherman. He escaped again, taking refuge with Zarenian smugglers.
### Introduction

The son of a Zarenian slave, Brandon speaks little about the years he spent at the beck-and-call of his Alanuan master. When a friend accidentally set the master's bedroom on fire while lighting a candle, fearful of the severe whipping his friend would no doubt receive, Brandon lashed out when they came to collect them. Because of his reaction, Brandon's master decided his "rebellious" nature was unsuitable for a slave and disposed of him. At eight years old, he was to be executed by drifting. With his arms and legs bound to a block of wood, he was set adrift to sea.

He was eventually picked up a fishermen who took full advantage of him. Brandon worked a few months as on the man's fishing boat until he met a group of smugglers trying to escape capture by Guardians. He offered to lead them off the island in return for giving him refuge. The smugglers, like most other Zarenians, weren't fond of the Alanuans' self-proclaimed superiority. [[Kody Renshaw|Captain Renshaw]] was impressed by Brandon's sheer luck and took to mentoring the boy, teaching him how to fish, hunt, defend himself, and other basic skills, all of which Brandon was eager to learn. Unfortunately for Captain Renshaw, Brandon never quite mastered his fear of water. The captain did love a good swim.

But as things always went with him, all good things came to an end. A [[The Guardians of Alanue|Guardian]] ship ambushed the smuggler's camp near the Zarenian gulf and arrested them all. Brandon was able to avoid them only because he had stormed off after an argument with Captain Renshaw. He fled into the mountains. His deepest regret.

He would later encounter the Caronas on their move to their new land. He stole their food at first, until the Caronas began leaving out food for him so he wouldn't have to "steal". This went on until Brandon had enough courage to hold short conversations with them. He got along splendidly with their son, Jorden, who hated that they were moving. Brandon empathized with Jorden to an extent. When they arrived at their new home, Jorden asked Brandon if he wanted to stay, which he agreed.

Brandon had a hard time interacting with the local youth. "Play" had never been his forte having worked all his life. He was eventually able to befriend Lina who, with everlasting patience, taught him how to relax. Brandon, in turn, regaled the others with stories of his adventures. At first, none of them could tell whether or not he was exaggerating. Then Brandon engineered a scheme to get them on a fishing boat and take them all sailing—and succeeded. No one doubted his stories after that. He also received a lifetime ban from the fishing port for his stunt.

A few years after he joined the Caronas, Daniel was born. His first actual "baby" brother, he took his new responsibility to the point Daniel takes Brandon's reprimands seriously.

He and Lina would eventually become engaged.

If you ask Brandon nowadays how he and his wife are doing, he will offer a smile and say, "We're good." What he doesn't mention is Lina's death was his main reason for joining the Promise, plus the "nearly dying" the three other times and a few other things.

On an afternoon out with his brothers, they ran across an arguing trio of Spirits: Espor, Curtidagua, and Diaroca. The recent construction of a nearby estate wiped out the creek the Water Spirit Curtidagua lived in. In her desperation, she found Diaroca's den and took it over. Diaroca didn't want his newly dug cave flooded out. Espor lived nearby and wanted a peaceful solution for all of them (her). Brandon sympathized with Espor greatly: he always had to mediate his brothers' arguments. Otherwise when they escalated into physical aggression, he often became collateral damage. He befriended Espor, who became fond of his adventure stories.

Eventually, during a stupor of grief, Brandon made his way back to Alanue, under the alias "Chase". He runs into Princess Raewynn and inadvertently helped her avoid kidnappers. When asked what reward he wanted, Brandon asked to join the Alanuan Academy as a student, an intangible dream he once held had as a child. Surprised by his humility, the Princess agreed to sponsor his studies. If he gave fealty to the King and impressed the instructors within a 3-month period, he could continue his studies, an honor he duly accepted.

Academy life was difficult for a man who never had a structured education. He had mountainloads of books to read—and he never was a fast reader. He found himself spending much of his time at the library.

The Promise eventually located him and tasked him as a spy. Not only did Brandon send them articles on what he had learned, he copied pages out of library texts, stole the Pledges that Alanuans used as tools, and documented blueprints of technology Zarenians never had. He also wired the soldiers' barracks, the royal palace, and the Academy—all places laboriously built and maintained by Zarenian slaves—with explosives.

During a combat training session with one of his fellow students, Brandon discovered he could absorb and release raw natural energy if he was pushed to an emotional extreme. He calls this a "Suicidal Spirit Bomb" as 1) he has no control over this, and 2) ends up debilitated or unconscious.

_. . . cool stuff happens here. . ._

As the rebellion comes to a close, the rogue nation is left in ruins. Looting, crime, and desperation set in as the Promise avoids collapsing in on itself. The high commanders of the Promise request Brandon stand-in as the leader of the new nation, which he adamantly refuses.

It isn't until he comes across Penny, her nephew Atticus, and her niece Stefanie who scrape by selling fish that he understands how much  someone needed to step up for them. On the eve of the rebellion's anniversary, he is sworn in as the new Chief of Zarenia to wary-yet-hopeful citizens.

Initially, Brandon and Penny's relationship is relatively transactional. She supports him when his trauma response flares up. He helps her and the children settle into a home. He builds a rapport with Atticus and Stefanie and eventually marries Penny on their insistence.

Brandon plans to retire after 5 years of his service.
### Physicalities
Brandon is close to underweight, no thanks to a fast metabolism and lack of desire to eat. He weighs in at 120–130 lbs. He has a smallish, medium frame-size.

His hair is one of his lowest priorities and he keeps it short. Its walnut-colored threads have a mild wave which Brandon brushes to the side. Sunken tired eyes shift from dark to light gray depending on his mood. Hooded eyelids blink exhaustion from lack of sleep.

Brandon wears a simple white tunic with brown leather pants, occasionally topping it with a brown overcoat and cloack. He cares little for affluence.

On his admission into the Alanuan Academy, he was given a set of white-and-blue academic robes.
### Mentalities

- sucks at Enchantry.

- Hates to disappoint people he cares about.

Brandon is clever, inquisitive, and cursed with an alarming sense of justice. He has trouble keeping his mouth shut at even the slightest hint of disparity, which frequently lands him in disputes. His outbursts as a child wound up with him nearly killed. Knowing this, Brandon is all about making the first impression: he dresses modestly and cleanly; he speaks with quiet, firm assertiveness; and exhibits the etiquette necessary for the situation. People seek him out for help because he can bring about a resolution.

### Abilities
By Synchronizing with his Spirit Espor, Brandon can use bamboo as creatively as he likes. His most practical usage involves sprouting stalks of bamboo and moving through them either with their flexible stalks or swinging from them with rope made of bamboo fibers.

During his time in Alanue, Brandon enhanced his ability with string. Capturing opponents, drawing them closer, tossing them further, is all child's play to him.
