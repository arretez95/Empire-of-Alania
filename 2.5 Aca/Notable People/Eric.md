### Introduction
He hated uniforms. He hated orders. So why, when the Zarenian war swung into full force, did he offer to help? Was it his suppressed distaste for those in power? Or perhaps the screaming cries of parents and children torn apart by Alanuan policy? Or was it the brunet with the piercing blue eyes that caught his attention?

To private ears, Eric admits to all. Anything, to stay by that man's side.

### Physicalities
A barrel-chested, thickset man with dark-brown hair and olive skin.

### Mentalities
Manipulative, petty, loyal, honest; a combination of traits that somehow wound up with Eric taking the position of army general. Life in an orphanage was no easy task. Should he wish to eat, he had to know how to get food or money. Scams, thefts, blackmail, and coercion were standardfare in his childhood. He eventually was too much to handle for his poor orphanage and they ended up selling him to an Alanuan merchant where his spent his teenagehood in chains. This, he knew, was the start of his yearning for freedom. Closed off, private, and reserved, what he's up to isn't really your business, especially regarding personal matters. If you need to know, he'll tell you. If you press him, he's not against sparking a petty rivalry.

Zarenians don't often nose into other's relationships, and privacy is greatly valued through the entire country. Marriage is still encouraged, regardless of whom it's to. But even still, Eric has found his homosexuality uncomfortable to talk about.

### History
---

Eric is a member on the Zarenian [[The Council of Seven|Council]], appointed as the Chief Advisor of Defense.

Eric wishes he had a place to call home, but in all his travels, he hasn't found home anywhere.

Despite his Açene heritage, Eric is aware of the Zarenian rebel's source of frustration. He was the product of coercion, an Açene merchant to a Zarenian slave. Like many rebels, he was a runaway and wound up with the cause by accident. Unable to sustain himself via normal means, he turned to thievery, joining the mountain bandits that terrorized the local villages. One raid went awry when Zarenian rebels showed up and their newfound Spirits aiding them in quelling the violence. Eric was left behind, severely injured in the scuffle.

Still in a battle frenzy, Daniel was prepared to kill him as punishment for causing pain to the villagers. Jorden intervened and blocked Daniel's sword. Having lost his right arm, Eric was no longer a threat and was instead taken prisoner. In return for information on the bandits, newly elected Chief Brandon allowed him to live and took him to Redcker.

Eric found himself voluntarily giving the information to Jorden, as if in appreciation for saving his life. Though he could only walk the streets of Redcker in chains, he found solace listening to the townsfolk, their speech unhindered by suspicion, the childrens' screams of unbridled joy, an atmosphere he felt blessed to witness. Eric eventually found himself romancing Jorden, and in making his vow of loyalty to him, also swore his allegiance to Brandon's cause.

He would encounter Rokida after a routine patrol turned hazardous. She had a great appreciation for his stories, and in return for granting him her ability, he had to entertain her with stories.
