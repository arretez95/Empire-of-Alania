**Species:** [[Mineral Water Spirit]]
**Element:** <span style="color:#6390F0;">Water</span>

This blue-feathered seahawk wouldn't describe herself as "loyal" despite carrying out most of [[Daniel Carona|Daniel's]] requests. She calls it having a "vested interest" in a friend's well-being. Birds of prey don't have many friends.

Once the inhabitant of a nameless mountain spring, she lost her home when it was drained  to build a new manor. Curti found a tree and flooded its roots to recreate her pond. Unbeknownst to her, the root tunnel system was claimed by [[Diaroca]]. Infuriated by her carelessness, Diaroca tried to shoot her down, and their fight escalated until Espor  intervened. Espor was unable to convince them to compromise—find Curti a tree with a waterfront, or get Diaroca back into the tunnels it so painstakingly dug—until the Carona brothers stepped in.

When [[Daniel]] convinced her to blast Jorden  with water and set all of them laughing, she agreed to be friends. Daniel, in turn, promised to find her a new home.

Curti would eventually realize home didn't have to by any fixed location. She's happy sitting on Daniel's shoulder.
