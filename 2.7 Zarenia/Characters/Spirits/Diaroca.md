**Species:** Mineral Water Spirit
**Element:** <span style="color:#E2BF65;">Earth</span>

If you feed it potatoes, it'll sprout potatoes from its head. If you feed it a carrot, it'll turn orange. If you feed it oats, it will shoot oat grains at you.

That's what [[Jorden]] and Diaroca spent a lot of the early part of their relationship doing: experimenting with the purposes of food.

Diaroca has spent most of his life consuming rock and dirt up until he ran into Jorden and his brothers. Sure, he's eaten leaves and roots before... but these were different!
