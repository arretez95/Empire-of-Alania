### Abilities
When in Sync with Curtidagua, Daniel is able to mold water into whatever shapes he likes. The larger or the more complex the shape, the more Spiritual Power required.

| **Ability**            | **Description**                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Water Orb              | generates a sphere of water; Daniel's most basic and fundamental ability                                                               |
| Water Craft            | allows Water Orb Shaping                                                                                                               |
| Water Soles            | by emitting Spiritual Power from his feet, Daniel can walk on water. This gives him additional mobility by floating on his Water Orbs. |
| Moon Pools             | By forming two pools of water, Daniel can teleport him and a small number of people a limited distance. Not a power he can use often.  |
| Temperature Absorption | changes the temperature of his water spells, allowing him to form ice or scalding water.                                               |
