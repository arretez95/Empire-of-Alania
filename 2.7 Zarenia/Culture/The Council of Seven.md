## Background
The **Council of Seven** is the executive leadership and cabinet of the Zarenian Federation. Its seven members serve as the collective head of government of Zarenia with each Councilor heading one of six executive departments. The chieftain serves as the representative head of state.

The Council is elected for a term of four years by their constituents and are not term-limited.

## Structure
### Executive
#### Chieftain
The executive leadership and representative head of Zarenia. After the Council elections, the Council elects one of its members as Chieftain. The chieftain chairs the Council and presides over executive meetings. They are only authorized to vote to break a tie. The chieftain exercises full authority in situations where council decisions cannot be made in time.

The chieftain can make self-appointed secretarial positions:
**Chief's Sword** — the Chief's right-hand-man, chief of staff.
**Chief's Shield** — the Chief's left-hand-man, security.

#### Deputy Chief
*Second-in-command*. Necessary in situations where the Chief is unavailable. Has no executive authority except in emergent cases where the Chief is absent. Positions are appointed by the Chief with Council approval.

#### Councilor
Each region's representative is elected by popular vote. As executives, each Councilor heads an advisory committee but cannot directly influence the committee, acting as presider and instilling decorum. Legislation is brought to the Council by the Advisory committee for passing or vetoing.

Only if the Council reaches a gridlock regarding the status of a bill is the Chieftain is allowed to partake in the voting process.

Once a bill has been approved, the Chieftain signs it into law.

Challenges to the law are brought before the Council.

#### Advisory Committees
Advisory committees submit legislation for various programs, agencies, and other administrative activities necessary to run the country. 
**Department of Defense and Security** — oversees defense operations.
**Department of Trade & Transportation** — monitors and regulates inter- and intranational trade and commerce.
**Department of Innovation and Technology** — oversees and manages research and technological outputs.
**Department of Agriculture and Environment** — regulates policy regarding agriculture, logging, mining, and developmental policies.
**Committee of Finance** — monitors and regulates government expenditures.
**Committee on Foreign Affairs** — leads foreign-policy and safeguards national interests.
