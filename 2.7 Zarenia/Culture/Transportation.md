Transportation in Zarenia was relatively primitive and relied on foot-power until the discovery of spirits and the use of their abilities became more well-known.
### Zipline
First developed for use by the [[Oathkeepers|Promise of Freedom]], these ropes zigzag across mountain and hilltops. Weaved between specially-grown trees shaped into a landing platform, ziplines help mountain-dwellers descend easily.

Lesser known to the public, these cables are infused with natural energy, allowing [[Magic Style — Spirit Power|Spiriters]] to move quickly between fixed points. Each node is also connected to the underground tunnel network in the case the aboveground system is damaged. Though not instantaneous, by spiriting through ziplines, a person can traverse significant distances over the span of a few hours.
