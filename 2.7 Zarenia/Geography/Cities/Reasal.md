Nestled on the rugged coastline of a towering mountain is a village with an air of mystique. Its weather-worn houses of stone stand stoically against the whims of time. Frenzied waves crash against rocky cliffs, a constant reminder of the village's isolation from the outside world. This is a place untouched by modernity, where tradition and secrecy intertwine.

Yet behind its picturesque façade lies unspoken hostility. Residents cast wary glances and hold their tongues, guarding themselves from prying eyes. The rumblings of deep-rooted mistrust echo through the cobblestone streets. Visitors are met with a frosty reception, silent disapproval that hangs heavy in the air.

People here have a sense of self-sufficiency, and by enduring the unforgiving and treacherous terrain, they embrace the solitude of their existence.

Here, secrets remain sacred and boundaries steadfastly maintained.
